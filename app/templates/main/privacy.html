Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026. This is a student capstone project; treat this as a good-faith policy, not legal advice.

What we collect

Only what's needed to personalize your learning path: your name and email for your account, and the Learning DNA you provide during onboarding (stream, interests, career goal, learning style, availability, language and budget preference). We do not collect unnecessary personal data.

What we don't do

We never sell your data. We never fabricate statistics about you or share your profile with third parties for advertising.

Cookies & sessions

We use a secure session cookie to keep you logged in. No third-party tracking cookies are installed by the platform itself.

Your choices

You can update your Learning DNA at any time, and you can request account deactivation by contacting an administrator.

Recommendations are informational

All personalization and recommendations are informational aids. You should independently evaluate any course, resource or career decision.
