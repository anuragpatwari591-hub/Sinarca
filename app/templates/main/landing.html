Frequently asked questions

Is NAVORA free?

Yes — the core platform, roadmaps, and Free-First discovery are free. Some future premium features are marked "Coming Soon".

Do you fabricate ratings or reviews?

Never. If rating data isn't available for a resource, we show "Rating unavailable" rather than inventing one.

How does the recommendation engine work?

It's an explainable, weighted scoring model — not a black-box AI. See our architecture notes.

What if a link is broken or a course is outdated?

Use "Report Resource" on any resource page — our admin team reviews every report.
